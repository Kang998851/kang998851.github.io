Products

Three desktop apps

They run on your computer, not in this browser. The site explains what they do. It is not an editor, a learning system, or an admin console.

Learn

Kang Learn

Enter a study scope and a number of days. The app builds tasks, tests, and stages. You import your own materials. Clear stages to earn ink points and redeem pens, notebooks, and postcards in the app. This site does not offer courses.

Office

Kang Office

Templates and workflows for everyday office work: meeting notes, weekly reports, quotes, and to-dos, so you spend less time reformatting and chasing follow-ups.

Data

Kang Data

Clean, classify, and export spreadsheets and loose files. Made for lists, bills, and archives, so you copy and paste less.
